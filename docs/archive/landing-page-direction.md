# Landing page implementation direction

> **Rejected by the user on 14 November 2026.** The boundaries and visual choices below are historical. The [boutique redesign plan](boutique-redesign-plan.md) replaces this direction for proposed new work.

This implements the revised one-page plan, not a rebrand. Visitor mode: Persuade. Primary action: start a WhatsApp enquiry; email and phone are alternatives. The business focus and contact details are user-confirmed.

**First viewport:** A quiet text wordmark and short navigation, followed by a large, plum serif heading explicitly naming blouse and dress making. Introductory copy and a WhatsApp link share the opening composition. A small service-area line gives visitors the local context immediately. No product imagery or invented mark.

**Reading sequence:** Introduction, short about passage, three compact service descriptions in open rows, a three-step customisation conversation, then a plum contact section with actual contact details and Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. No product grids, empty controls or unrelated content.

**Interaction:** Ordinary anchor navigation and outbound WhatsApp, email and phone links. No animation, menu overlay, form or JavaScript. A skip link and visible keyboard focus are required. Contact details remain selectable and copyable.

**Layout:** Restrained asymmetric desktop composition with generous separation and left-aligned text; one reading column on phones, with no fixed heights or horizontal overflow. Contact rows wrap naturally for long addresses.

**Provisional palette:** Paper #FBF8F5, plum #462238, deep plum #35182A, muted plum #6B5662, rose #F2E5E8, antique gold #9A7135. These are temporary implementation tokens based on the earlier plan's named colour families, not extracted or approved V4 values. Gold is decorative only. No sourced font files exist; use Georgia for display and the system sans-serif stack for body copy as allowed by the reviewed plan.

**Boundaries:** Build directly from the settled page sequence and brand families; do not reopen identity exploration. No image comps or generated logo. Keep pending domain and approved assets explicit in developer documentation. Draft stays local and noindex until release details are ready.
